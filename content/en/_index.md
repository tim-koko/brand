## Brand Story

### We are Tim & Koko!

Our name is a tribute to Tim Berners-Lee, the inventor of the World Wide Web, and Koko, the female gorilla who communicated effortlessly in sign language. The name stands for technology and communication at eye level — just like our work and our spirit.

## Our Drive

**The potential of modern technologies, made tangible**

We believe that modern technological approaches like Cloud Native and Open Source are the foundation for a better future. That they make a noticeable difference for companies and people. That's why we help make Cloud Native Computing accessible to everyone. So that everyone can experience its power and magic for themselves.

## Our Attitude

![Tim&Koko team](/images/unsere-marke/tk_7.jpg)

### Openness wins

We keep our eyes and ears open and engage with our clients and partners. Open source and open standards are second nature to us. Because anyone who isn't open to new things and other people doesn't get very far. Going it alone or sticking to well-trodden paths rarely offers inspiration or room to grow.

### Only those who act, move things forward

We like to stay pragmatic and get things done. Because passion and good ideas alone aren't enough. Anyone who wants to make real progress and solve problems for good has to act. And that also means trying or daring something new sometimes.

### People come first, then technology

For us, people always come first — with all their needs and possibilities. Because technology for its own sake helps no one. What matters is using technical know-how and technical solutions in a way that actually helps clients or partners. So that together we can create something new.

### Quality comes from joy

We make sure that work with us brings joy to everyone. Because those who are passionate about what they do don't just enjoy it more — they're also more successful. Deeper engagement and constant learning make you automatically better — not just as an individual, but as a team.

## Character

Our brand story tells the story of the company and, at its core, sums up the key elements that define the brand. It serves to communicate the brand's identity and purpose clearly and concisely.

### 1. We always see the good and inspire

We love what we do. And we always see the glass as half full. We treat other people and their work with appreciation. With our positive attitude and enthusiasm, we inspire everyone around us: our colleagues, clients and partners.

### 2. We say what we think and offer answers

We're not afraid to be honest and get straight to the point. We show new perspectives and paths. Our honesty is refreshing, but never inappropriate or negative.

### 3. We don't take ourselves too seriously and get a little crazy sometimes

We're ambitious and work extremely professionally. But we can laugh at ourselves and are always easy to deal with. We like the unconventional and enjoy surprising people. A little craziness is just part of who we are.

### 4. We stay current without losing touch

We know our field and are often a step ahead. New things fascinate us, and that's where we feel at home. The same goes for building bridges or reducing complexity. We make things accessible and bring people along with us.
