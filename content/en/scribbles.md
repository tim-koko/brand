## Overview

The scribble-style visuals give the brand a relaxed character and support the visual language with their playful aesthetic. They convey information in a charming way and give the brand a distinctive recognition value.

![Scribble icons](/images/scribbles/icons.svg)

## Usage

The graphic elements are used deliberately on covers, homepages, boxes and promotional surfaces. They are applied at large scale, in the background of layout areas. They can either be placed as individual cropped shapes, or repeated as a pattern. The shapes may be rotated freely.
