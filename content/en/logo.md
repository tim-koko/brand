## Clear space

The clear space guarantees the logo's legibility and impact by isolating it from competing visual elements such as text, graphics and images. No accompanying text, logos, graphics etc. should appear within this zone.

{{< img src="/images/logo/Schutzzone.svg" alt="Clear space" size="medium" >}}

The height of the clear space is derived from the lowercase letter "o" and defines the minimum distance for the protected area around the logo.

## Minimum size

The logo was carefully designed to remain legible even at small sizes. At large scale there is no upper limit, but caution is required at smaller sizes.

{{< img src="/images/logo/Mindestgrösse.svg" alt="Minimum size" size="small" >}}

**Minimum size print:** X = 16mm

**Minimum size digital:** X = 24px
