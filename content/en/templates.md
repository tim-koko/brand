## Paper

We use a single "corporate paper" for all print materials. The big advantage is that every application can be produced on the same paper — whether it's a short digital-print run, a high-quality offset-printed brochure, or self-printed handouts from the office laser printer.

**Our corporate paper: Superset Snow (matte)**

Matte, smooth super-white, wood-free, FSC certified.

Superset Snow is a versatile, high-quality paper from Fischer Papier. Its velvety smooth surface and excellent print results make Superset compelling. Thanks to its special surface finish, it prints excellently and feels pleasant to the touch. An outstanding natural paper that delivers fantastic print results.
